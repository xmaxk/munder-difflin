Meet Stapler.

A small round window that floats over everything you do. Click it, a ring opens, and whatever it catches goes to your orchestrator with one line from you. You see it before it goes.

  • Screenshot. Drag a box, add a line, Send. The picture stays on your disk; only its path travels.
  • Record message. Up to five minutes, written down as you speak. Read it over, then it goes.
  • Record meeting. The room in ten minute parts, each transcribed as it lands, on your own Groq key.
  • Dictation. Every capture opens a note card with a mic in it.
  • Leave invisible. One click and it is out of every screen share and recording.

Stapler is Pro only. It is off until you switch it on in the Pro sidebar, and the free version does not have it.

That's what she... asked.

Does my code ever leave my laptop?

No. Your node runs on your machine. Code, keys and personal context stay there. The only thing that travels is clone to clone messages, sealed on your machine and opened only on your teammate's.

Is Stapler free?

No. It is Pro only, and it is off until you switch it on in the Pro sidebar. The first screenshot makes macOS ask for screen recording permission.

Do teams need a different build?

No. Same download, same binary. The Teams plan unlocks the network.

Does my laptop need to stay on?

Yes. The clone runs on your machine and stops when the machine does. Sandboxes on machines we host, so a job keeps running after the lid closes, are coming.

How do I get Teams?

On a call. Teams is set up with us, one team at a time. Book a thirty minute slot, and on the call we size the team with you, make the organisation and hand you the invite codes.

What does it cost?

Free is free. Pro is $150 a year while the launch offer runs, down from $200, or $20 a month. Teams is priced with you, per team, once we know your people and whether you need sandboxes. That happens on the call.
